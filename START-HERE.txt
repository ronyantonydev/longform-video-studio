HOW TO MAKE A VIDEO
===================

ONE TIME
 1. Double-click  setup.command  and follow the questions.
 2. Drop 3-5 videos whose style you want to copy into  style-examples/ .

EACH VIDEO
 1. Open Claude in this folder and type:  start a new video called israel-desert-farming
 2. Open  videos/israel-desert-farming/brief.txt  and fill in the topic, length, notes.
 3. Type:  make the video in israel-desert-farming
 4. Claude will stop three times and ask "OK?" (script, look, final video).
 5. Your finished files appear in the same folder:
      video.mp4, thumbnail.png, youtube-description.txt, captions.srt, cost.txt

FEEDBACK
 Write comments in that video's  feedback.txt  (for example "too slow at the start").
 Claude fixes that video and asks before saving a lesson for future videos.

GET THE LATEST VERSION
 Double-click  update.command . Your videos, keys and lessons are never touched.

Do not open or edit the hidden .engine folder.
