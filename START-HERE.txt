HOW TO MAKE A VIDEO
===================

ONE TIME
 1. Mac: double-click  setup.command . Linux: run  bash setup.command .
 2. Connect your Chrome to Claude (Claude in Chrome extension).

ADD A CHANNEL (once per channel)
 1. Open Claude in this folder and type:  set up channel my-channel
 2. Drop 3-5 videos whose style this channel should copy into
      channels/my-channel/style-examples/
 3. Type:  learn the style of my-channel

MAKE A VIDEO
 Type:  make a video for my-channel about desert farming in Israel, 25 minutes
 Claude stops twice and asks "OK?":
   - the script (check the facts)
   - the final video
 Finished files appear in  channels/my-channel/videos/<topic>/ :
   video.mp4, thumbnail.png, youtube-description.txt, captions.srt, cost.txt

FEEDBACK
 Write comments in that video's  feedback.txt , or just tell Claude.
 Claude fixes that video and asks before saving a lesson for the channel.

CHANNEL SETTINGS
 Each channel has  channel-rules.md  (style, images, sources, voice, budget).
 Claude fills it in; you can change any line.

GET THE LATEST VERSION
 Say  update Video Studio  (Mac: or double-click  update.command ). Your channels, videos, keys and lessons are never touched.

Do not open or edit the hidden .engine folder.
